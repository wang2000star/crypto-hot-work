05 · SCOPE & METHOD

How to read these results

A transparent first-pass map, built to make follow-up review easier.

Sources & scope

ePrint records plus ASIACRYPT, CiC, CRYPTO, EUROCRYPT, JoC, PKC, RWC, TCC, TCHES, and ToSC. CHES maps to TCHES; FSE maps to ToSC. Collection window: 2022–2026 year-to-date.

Classification

Explicit keyword rules assign specific directions from titles, abstracts, and keywords. Papers may receive multiple labels. 2,936 of 11,438 title-deduplicated paper records were assigned a label.

Coverage limits

Some venue records lack abstracts. RWC contributes talks, not proceedings papers. 2026 is incomplete and ASIACRYPT/TCC 2026 had not met by the collection date. Review labels and venue-year completeness before drawing strong conclusions.
